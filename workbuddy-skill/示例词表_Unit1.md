achievement | 成就；功绩

## 句
He devoted his whole life to the cause. | 他把一生都奉献给了这项事业。
Her achievement was acknowledged all over the world. | 她的成就得到了全世界的承认。
After 190 failures, she did not abandon the research. | 190 次失败之后，她没有放弃这项研究。
She decided to extract the plant with cool water. | 她决定用冷水提取这种植物。
